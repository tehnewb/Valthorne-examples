Valthorne: @DEMO_ID@

Extract the entire ZIP, then double-click Start.bat.
Java, the engine and assets are included. No installation or Gradle is needed.
Keep bin, lib and runtime beside Start.bat. Saves and captures go under build.

Guide and controls: https://github.com/tehnewb/Valthorne-examples/blob/main/docs/@DEMO_ID@.md
From PowerShell: .\Start.bat --smoke (bounded check), or .\Start.bat --help.
FPS: click Enter arena to play; Escape pauses and releases the mouse.

Windows x64 and a compatible graphics driver are required. Advanced demos need
OpenGL 4.3. If startup fails, the console keeps its error visible.

Code: Apache-2.0 (LICENSE). Assets/dependencies: THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md and notices
inside lib/*.jar. Bundled Java: runtime/legal and java-runtime.properties.
Matching Java source: https://github.com/tehnewb/Valthorne-examples/releases/tag/v@VERSION@
